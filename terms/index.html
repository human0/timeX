Auto-Renewable Subscriptions

Last updated: July 2026

Time X offers optional auto-renewable memberships that renew monthly until cancelled. Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your device account settings.

Gold Monthly

  • Title: Gold Monthly (App Store product: sub_gold_monthly)
  • Length: 1 month, auto-renewing
  • Price: Shown in the app at purchase in your local currency
  • Includes: Daily time allowance (1 hour per day) and Golden community access

Platinum Monthly

  • Title: Platinum Monthly (App Store product: sub_platinum_monthly)
  • Length: 1 month, auto-renewing
  • Price: Shown in the app at purchase in your local currency
  • Includes: Monthly time allowance (up to 8 hours per day) and VIP priority access
