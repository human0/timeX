Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Time Xchange (Pty) Ltd ("Time X", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Time X mobile app and website at timexchange.co.za.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, complete your profile, or communicate with us. This may include:

  • Name, email address, and contact information
  • Profile information and service descriptions
  • Communication preferences and in-app messages
  • Subscription and purchase status (processed through Apple App Store or Google Play; we do not store payment card details)
  • Location data when you choose to share it for nearby services
  • Photos you upload for your profile
  • Advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID / Apple Identifier for Advertisers) when ads are shown and you have not limited ad tracking
  • Device and usage information used to serve and measure ads (for example ad interactions and approximate location derived by ad partners)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Manage memberships and in-app purchases
  • Send technical notices, support messages, and push notifications you opt into
  • Respond to your comments and questions
  • Protect against fraudulent or illegal activity
  • Show advertisements (including personalized ads where permitted) to free and non-entitled users

Advertising (AdMob)

Time X uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads to users who are not on an entitled Gold or Platinum membership. AdMob and its partners may collect and process advertising identifiers and related device/usage data to deliver, personalize, and measure ads, and to combat fraud and abuse.

Where required (for example in the EEA/UK), we present a consent form before personalized ads. On iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt may also appear when tracking is requested. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example Reset/Delete Advertising ID on Android, or App Tracking / Limit Ad Tracking on iOS). Entitled Gold and Platinum members do not see third-party ads.

Google’s advertising practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob documentation. Our developer app-ads.txt lists authorized ad sellers for Time X.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data with service providers that help us operate the app (such as hosting, authentication, and advertising partners like Google AdMob), when required by law, or with your consent. Advertising partners receive advertising identifiers and related signals only as needed to serve and measure ads under applicable consent and platform rules.

Subscriptions and Payments

Auto-renewable subscriptions are billed through Apple or Google. Payment processing is subject to the respective store’s terms. See Auto-Renewable Subscriptions for plan details.

Your Choices

You may update your profile, manage notification preferences in the app, limit ad tracking in your device settings, or contact us to request account or data deletion at support@timexchange.co.za.

Data Privacy and App Store Compliance

Our app complies with App Store and Google Play Store privacy requirements:

  • We collect data needed for app functionality and, where applicable, advertising
  • User data is encrypted and securely stored
  • We share data with third parties (such as Google AdMob for ads) only as described in this Privacy Policy and with required consent where applicable
  • Users can request data deletion at any time

Camera and Location Access

Camera access is requested for profile photo uploads, service documentation and verification processes. Location access is requested for finding nearby service providers, local community matching and service area verification. Both are optional and can be denied or revoked at any time through device settings.

Contact
