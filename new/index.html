FAQ

Good questions.

What members ask before their first booking.

Who can join?

Anyone. Time Xchange is built for professionals, and open to everyone: households, students, teachers and community groups. Every member’s time counts the same way.

Do I have to trade with the same person who helped me?

No. Time you earn from one member can be spent with anyone in the network. Most time changes hands several times: an accountant pays an architect, who pays a photographer, who pays an engineer.

What if a session runs over?

The requester chooses: stop at the reserved time, or allow paid overtime from their available balance.

Do I need to earn time before I can book?

No. Every plan includes an allowance, so you can book your first session straight away.

What can I pay with?

Time, for most bookings. When both sides agree, you can add cash on top, for example to cover materials or a specialist rate, or a real gift to say thanks, like gold, wine, vouchers, books, shoes or food.

Are there hidden fees?

No hidden fees between members: you pay only what you agree, in time, plus any cash or gift. Memberships are billed separately through the App Store and Google Play. [Confirm the fee wording before launch.]

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. You can cancel or reschedule in the app. If you cancel a booking that has started, any unpaid work goes to the provider and the rest of the reserved time returns to you.
